# domain/catalog

**Owns:** schema `catalog.*` — `fares` (route/cabin/price catalogue, with the trip each price is for), `airports` (IATA reference for pins + autocomplete, with a destination's one line), `demand_daily` (UTC-day search counts, no member id), `place_photos` (the photo shown for each airport's city: addresses and credit, or a satellite view's centre — never an image; ADR-IMPL-043), `place_landmarks` (up to three landmarks of each airport's city: their photographs' addresses and credits, never an image; ADR-IMPL-064), `source_holds` (a Wikimedia source that answered 429, until its Retry-After; ADR-IMPL-064), and `site_places` / `site_routes` (the company site's cities, their airports and the US airports the site lists a price from — a nightly snapshot; ADR-IMPL-045).
**Publishes:** none on the journal. When `catalog.search_events` is on and `KAFKA_BROKERS` is set, `GET /v1/search` notes a route event that a flusher publishes to `bbc.search.v1`.
**Consumes:** `bbc.search.v1` on the worker (`APP_ROLE` not `api`), group `catalog.demand`, idempotent via `platform.kafka_processed`.
**Ports:** none (`needs: []`). Repository surface is the port — a Sabre / company-API adapter can replace `fares.repo` later without route changes. Outside calls: the `resolve-place-photos` job asks Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons and (with `PEXELS_API_KEY`) Pexels; `resolve-place-landmarks` asks Wikidata and Commons; `sync-site-destinations` asks the company's site (`buybusinessclass.com/api` and its `/storage/`; ADR-IMPL-062); `scripts/load-pricing-rules.ts --check-site` asks the site's search (`webapi.buybusinessclass.com/flights/search`, `pricing/site-check.ts`); `catalogModule({ fetch, siteClock })` lets tests answer for them, and pace the site's API without waiting.
**Facade:** `searchFares` (at most 30), `getFare`, `destinations`, `searchAirports`, `getAirport`, `getAirports`, `indicativeFor` (the estimate an undated search shows for a route, or null — requests asks it for a quote, ADR-IMPL-042), `importCsv`.
**Routes:** `GET /v1/search` (rate rule `search`; only fares on sale now — published, `valid_from ≤ now < valid_until`, as `/v1/fares/:id` reads them; `when` is the travel day: a timed fare by its departure day in the origin's zone, an undated one while still sold that day; a `when` before yesterday or past two years ahead lists nothing, without a query; `when` not a date or `from`/`to` not three letters → 400 — ADR-IMPL-041 amendment), `GET /v1/fares/:id` (410 if past `valid_until` or unpublished, 404 for an id that is not a UUID, rate rule `read`), `GET /v1/airports?q=` (max 8, rate rule `read`), `GET /v1/airports/popular?from=` and `GET /v1/airports/home-suggestion?tz=` (rate rule `read`), `GET /v1/places/photos?codes=` (1–20 IATA codes, rate rule `read`), `GET /v1/places/:code/landmarks` (rate rule `read`; ETag, 304; 404 for a code that is no airport — ADR-IMPL-064), `POST /v1/internal/catalog/import` (`catalog:import`), `PUT` and `DELETE /v1/internal/places/:code/photo` (`catalog:import`), `GET /v1/internal/demand?days=7` (`ops:read`, no rate limit).
**Jobs:** `expire-fares` every 15 minutes — sets `published = false` where `valid_until < now()`, then clears the destinations cache. `demand-rollup` at 03:15 UTC writes yesterday's sketches into `demand_daily`. `resolve-place-photos` every minute — up to ten due cities (ADR-IMPL-043), its requests ending within 40 s of its start (each may take 8 s; timeout 50 s): a city it has not reached by then waits for the next run, uncounted (ADR-IMPL-064). `resolve-place-landmarks` every five minutes — up to two due places, timeout 240 s, a place starting only while its slowest look (162 s: two landmark questions, the city found again, two Commons questions) can end within 225 s of the run's start, else waiting untouched (`later`); nothing with `catalog.landmarks` off or a Wikimedia source held (ADR-IMPL-064). `sync-site-destinations` at 07:40 UTC — the site's cities, their airports and photos (ADR-IMPL-045); nothing asked with `catalog.site_photos` and `catalog.site_pins` off. The site's API allows 120 requests a minute: its ~241 pages start 600 ms apart across the four workers, a 429 holds them all for its `Retry-After` (60 s without one, at most 120 s) and the page is asked once more, a second 429 fails the night; photo checks are not paced. Three to five minutes a night, up to about seven when staging's night reads the site from the same host (the 120 are the address's); timeout 600 s (ADR-IMPL-062). The site's photo rows are known by either address, today's (`SITE_STORAGE`) or `weba.`'s (`LEGACY_SITE_STORAGE`): the night moves an old one or hands it back.
**Home map:** `destinations()` is `DISTINCT ON (route_to)` ordered by price. Without Redis, a 60s process cache. With Redis, key `catalog:dest:{generation}:{HOME}` and `INCR catalog:dest:gen` on import and expire-fares, so every home misses without a SCAN. Airports use `catalog:airports:gen` the same way. A fare that expires mid-minute can stay on the map for ≤ 60s; its detail answers 410. After the fares' pins, with `catalog.site_pins` and estimates on, up to twelve of the site's cities listed from home (`site_routes`), priced by the formula in the member's cabin and cached the same way (`catalog:site:{generation}:{HOME}:{cabin}:{rules}`); the sync bumps the generation (ADR-IMPL-045).
**Estimates:** `GET /v1/search` adds `estimate` (ADR-IMPL-037) when `catalog.estimates` is on and an undated search has no fare in the cabin: the company's formula, with the rules from the flag row `catalog.pricing_rules` (written by `scripts/load-pricing-rules.ts`; never in the repository; `--check-site` compares a rule set with the site's search on 24 routes and prints counts, never a price; it passes only when no route differs and every route got the site's business figure). Continents are OurAirports' by country, except where the site prices a country elsewhere: Cyprus, St Barthélemy and Saint Helena as Europe, Timor-Leste as Oceania; Turkey is Asia, as on the site (ADR-IMPL-062). Missing, hand-edited or unreadable rules → no estimate and `bbc_search_estimates_unavailable{reason}` (`missing`, `invalid`, `error`: the row could not be read, logged `pricing rules unreadable`); shown ones count in `bbc_search_estimates_shown{cabin}`. `indicativeFor` applies the same flag, rules, formula and fare rule for a quote request whose app showed the estimate, returns the rules' fingerprint with it, and counts missing or hand-edited rules in `bbc_request_estimates_unavailable{reason}` (the site's pins, ADR-IMPL-045, in `bbc_home_estimates_unavailable{reason}`) (the requests module counts what it stores, `bbc_request_estimates_stored{cabin}`); with estimates off, or no valid rules, it reads nothing. A killed catalog still answers it: `catalog.estimates` off is the switch.
**Discovery** (ADR-IMPL-039): `popular` returns at most four destinations from `from` — the routes searched at least 5 times in the last seven UTC days (`demand_daily`, all cabins together), busiest first, then the hubs by `popularity`; never the origin or an airport under 100 km from it. Names only: a count never leaves the server. With Redis, cached 5 minutes under `catalog:popular:{airports gen}:{FROM}`. Until `catalog.search_events` is on and `demand-rollup` has run, it shows the hubs alone. `bbc_popular_destinations_shown{source="searches"|"hubs"}` counts what was shown. `home-suggestion` returns the busiest airport whose `tz` is the phone's zone or that zone's current name (`ZONE_ALIASES`: Android reports ICU names such as `Asia/Calcutta`) — a tie on popularity goes to the zone's own city (`America/Lima` → LIM, not Arequipa; its airports by code), and a tie between other cities, none the zone's own, suggests nothing (`null`, onboarding asks); `null` for UTC or an offset; 400 when `tz` is not a time zone.
**Place photos** (ADR-IMPL-043): `GET /v1/places/photos` answers from `place_photos` at once — `photo` (a card's and a full-width address, and the credit: author, licence, the photo's page; `club` for an operator's photo, null when it needs none), `satellite` (the point the app centres a Mapbox satellite view on: the city's centre, else the airport) or `none` (not looked up yet: the app shows its own image); an unknown code is left out, and a known airport never asked about gets a `pending` row in the same request (one INSERT … SELECT; on conflict a no-op update returns the row another request added, so it is answered too). `resolve-place-photos` claims up to ten due rows (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, new rows first, a ten-minute hold), asks Wikidata once (P238 at any rank, so a demoted code such as KIV still counts → the airport within 50 km of ours → P931 the city: P3451 night view, P18 image, P625 centre; with no such airport, or one naming no city, the city by its own name near the airport — Wikidata's search, a settlement within 150 km: ours by its English name, else the search's first (its order: "Kishinev" finds Chișinău before the nearer Chișinău Ghetto), one more question each, two a run, a third deferred; a search that fails is its own city's failure, the cities still waiting for one deferred; a tab or another control character in a city's name is searched as a space: ADR-IMPL-064) and Commons twice (500 and 1280 px, Wikimedia's standard widths; JPEG, landscape, wider than 1280, free licence, no restrictions, an author when the licence asks), then Pexels for at most three cities (`<city> skyline`, a description naming the city and no person); else the satellite view. A failed source (Commons' HTTP-200 errors included) leaves the row as it was and tries again after `least(attempts, 24)` hours — never a downgrade —; a 429 from Wikidata or Commons is not a failure: the cities wait for its Retry-After, the look not counted, and the source is held in `source_holds` (a run that claims cities while a hold is on asks nothing and puts them back until it ends; ADR-IMPL-064) — and so does a claimed row whose airport does not come back (`withoutOutcome`, stage `airport`); a key Pexels refuses (401) counts as none for the run — a photo shown stays, a city without one gets a day of satellite view; every resolved row is looked at again after 30–33 days. An operator's photo (`PUT /v1/internal/places/:code/photo`) never expires and no job write touches it (`source IS DISTINCT FROM 'override'` on each one); `DELETE` hands the city back. Flag `catalog.place_photos` (seeded on) off: the route answers `{ items: [] }` and the job asks nothing. Metrics `bbc_place_photos_resolved{status,source}`, `bbc_place_photos_failed{stage}`, `bbc_place_photos_held{stage}`, `bbc_place_photos_requested`.
**Landmarks** (ADR-IMPL-064): `GET /v1/places/:code/landmarks` answers from `place_landmarks` at once — up to three `{ name, imageUrl, width, height, credit: { text, licence, licenceUrl? } }`, best first, without the items the flag's `skip` lists; none while `catalog.landmarks` (seeded off, `skip: ["Q177174"]`) is off. `resolve-place-landmarks` takes up to two due places — airports with a city photo row or the site's cities' main airports, new ones first — finds each city's Wikidata item (the one its rows know — looked up again when it answers nothing at all, as a merged item does — else as the city photo does; a search by name that fails stops the run with a `failed` marker for its place alone), asks WDQS once per city (items within 20 km of the city item's P625 that are a tourist attraction, a landmark or a museum by class, or heritage; no populated place, never the city; the forty with most sitelinks, with `hint:Query hint:optimizer "None"`; 65-s timeout) and Commons once at 1280 px, and keeps, among the first twenty, each item's first file — P18, else P3451, else P8592 — that is a JPEG at least 1600 px wide, 1.2–2.4 wide for 1 high, not named as a map, logo, flag or coat of arms, under ADR-IMPL-043's credit rules (`commonsCredit`), and neither the city's own photo nor another landmark's; three at most, fewer when fewer qualify. Rows per place: `landmark` at ranks 1–3; a `fewer` marker after the last one found (the job does not ask again for 30–33 days); a `failed` marker (rank 1) for a place no look has worked for yet (six hours). A 429 holds its source (`source_holds`) for its Retry-After and stops the run; another failure keeps what a place has, looks again in six hours and stops the run. Twice is the same rows (upsert by rank). Metrics `bbc_place_landmarks_resolved{found}`, `bbc_place_landmarks_failed{stage}`, `bbc_place_landmarks_held{stage}`.
**Trip and description** (ADR-IMPL-056, 0031): `fares.trip` (`catalog.fare_trip`: `round_trip` | `one_way`, default `one_way`) is what a fare's price is for — round trip only where the catalogue says so (images 04 and 13 print `round trip` beside a fare); the fares import's optional `trip` column says it per row, a file without the column or an empty cell says one way, and any other value refuses the import; the trip is part of a fare's identity (0034): a fare is one per route, cabin, carrier, valid_from and trip, so one route sold one way and round trip is two fares, and a fare without a carrier is one fare too (`fares_route_cabin_carrier`, UNIQUE NULLS NOT DISTINCT, the import's ON CONFLICT target; an empty carrier cell is none); `FareVM.trip` carries it. `airports.description` is a destination's one line under its city (04), null until the airports import's optional `description` column gives one (CHECK: 1–160 characters once trimmed; a longer cell refuses the import, an empty one clears the line, a file without the column keeps every airport's line); `AirportVM.description` carries it wherever an airport is served — the search's `from` and `to`, `/v1/airports`, `popular`, `home-suggestion`, and Home's `home` and pins (`DestinationPinVM`, the fares' and the site's) — and Home's ETag follows it.
**Import** (`POST /v1/internal/catalog/import`): the CSV is read as RFC 4180 (quoted commas, `""`, quoted line breaks; each record keeps the file line it starts on, the header is line 1). Every row is checked before anything is written, in one transaction: a bad file writes nothing and answers 400 `VALIDATION` with up to twenty `details` `{ path: "row N.<field>", message }`; a row the database still refuses is named `row N` with its constraint, never the query. Fares: route codes three letters, different, and airports the catalogue knows (one lookup); price and published price digits with at most two decimals, above 0, published ≥ price and with its source; currency three letters (upper-cased; empty is USD); an empty optional cell (carrier name, product, published price and source) is none; valid_until after valid_from; duration_minutes whole minutes above 0 (empty is none; CHECK `fares_duration_pos`, 0035); nonstop yes or no in any case (`TRUE`/`yes`/`y`/`1`, `FALSE`/`no`/`n`/`0`; empty is nonstop), anything else refused. Airports: code three letters, country code two, lat −90…90, lng −180…180 (CHECK `airports_coordinates`, 0035), popularity a whole number from 0 (empty is 0), region one the catalogue already uses (Home's keys), tz an IANA zone.
**Out of scope:** promotional offers (`proposals.offers`), Sabre adapter, mobile wiring.
**Invariants tested:** search JFK→LHR business → ≥3 · expired fare → 410 · airports `q=JF` ranks JFK · import idempotent, a fare without a carrier included · one route's one-way and round-trip fares are two fares · a fare is one way unless its import row says round trip, an unknown trip refuses the import · a destination's line reaches every airport answer and Home past its ETag, an import without the column keeps it, an empty cell clears it, over 160 characters refuses it (and the CHECK refuses a blank one) · unauthenticated → 401 · timed fixture `arrive − depart == duration` · fa01 maps to 18:55 / 07:00 / +1 · same clocks under Europe/Chisinau and America/Los_Angeles · every `airports.csv` `tz` is IANA. Search events reject `memberId`, `ip`, and `deviceId`. Estimates: only without fares, never between one metro's airports or for a country in `UNCONFIRMED_COUNTRIES` (empty since ADR-IMPL-062), first only above business; Istanbul priced as Asia; the engine reproduces the reference results (`tests/parity`, CI secret; the club's engine since ADR-IMPL-062). The site's API: one request start every 600 ms across the pool, a 429 retried once after its wait, a second one fails the read and stops its workers; a photo stored under the site's former address is moved or handed back. `--check-site`: counts and route codes only. Discovery: searched ≥ 5 times in seven days first, then the hubs · never the origin or its metro · no count in the body · `Asia/Calcutta` → DEL, `Etc/UTC` → none · every airport `tz` is reachable from the name a phone reports. Place photos: a new airport is `none` then a Commons photo with its credit · Pexels only with a key and at most three a run · a source that fails keeps the row and backs off · a claimed city whose airport does not come back backs off the same way · the job never overwrites an operator's photo · unknown codes are left out · flag off → no photos, no lookups · an item an older app cannot read is dropped, not the answer · a code matched at any rank (KIV and RMO find Chișinău) · a city found by its own name when Wikidata knows no airport near ours, never a namesake over 150 km away, by the search's order when no name is ours · a name with a tab searched by its words, neither job stopped by it · a failed search retries its own city only · a slow run starts no request that would end past 40 s, the cities left wait uncounted · a 429 defers the cities uncounted and holds the source; no run asks before the hold ends. Landmarks: the question's filters (20 km, sights or heritage, no Q56061 or P576 exclusion, never the city) · Paris and Chișinău from WDQS's and Commons' saved answers — portrait icons rescued by their aerial views, a 2.5-wide photo and an 848-px one refused · no duplicate image · the job twice writes the same rows · a merged city item is found again · a search by name that fails marks its own place, the other untouched · a place that cannot finish inside the run waits untouched · a 429 holds and stops · a failure keeps what a place has · fewer than three leaves a marker · a place that drops from three to one, then none, keeps no stale rank · the route's shape, ETag, 304 and 404, the flag and its `skip` · the CHECKs.
