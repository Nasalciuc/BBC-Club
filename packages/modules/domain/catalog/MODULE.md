# domain/catalog

**Owns:** schema `catalog.*` — `fares` (route/cabin/price catalogue, with the trip each price is for), `airports` (IATA reference for pins + autocomplete, with a destination's one line), `demand_daily` (UTC-day search counts, no member id), `place_photos` (the photo shown for each airport's city: addresses and credit, or a satellite view's centre — never an image; ADR-IMPL-043), `place_landmarks` (up to three landmarks of each airport's city: their photographs' addresses and credits, never an image; ADR-IMPL-064), `source_holds` (a Wikimedia source that answered 429, until its Retry-After; ADR-IMPL-064), `site_places` / `site_routes` (the company site's cities, their airports and the US airports the site lists a price from — a snapshot the sync replaces twice a day, each route served for a day after its read: `valid_until`, CHECK `site_routes_validity`; ADR-IMPL-045, ADR-IMPL-067), and `site_prices` (what the site's own search answered for a route and trip — its figures, whole USD, or `none`: the company has no price there — shown for at most 24 hours, CHECK `site_prices_expiry`; ADR-IMPL-063).
**Publishes:** none on the journal. When `catalog.search_events` is on and `KAFKA_BROKERS` is set, `GET /v1/search` notes a route event that a flusher publishes to `bbc.search.v1`.
**Consumes:** `bbc.search.v1` on the worker (`APP_ROLE` not `api`), group `catalog.demand`, idempotent via `platform.kafka_processed`.
**Ports:** none (`needs: []`). Repository surface is the port — a Sabre / company-API adapter can replace `fares.repo` later without route changes. Outside calls: the `resolve-place-photos` job asks Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons and (with `PEXELS_API_KEY`) Pexels; `resolve-place-landmarks` asks Wikidata and Commons; `sync-site-destinations` asks the company's site (`buybusinessclass.com/api` and its `/storage/`; ADR-IMPL-062); `scripts/load-pricing-rules.ts --check-site` asks the site's search (`webapi.buybusinessclass.com/flights/search`, `pricing/site-check.ts`); with `catalog.site_prices` on, an undated search without a stored answer asks it once (1 s) and `refresh-site-prices` asks it for the pins' routes (10 s each), through one client per process with a breaker (`application/site-prices.ts`, ADR-IMPL-063); `catalogModule({ fetch, siteClock })` lets tests answer for them, and pace the site's API, the price job and the breaker without waiting.
**Facade:** `searchFares` (at most 30), `getFare`, `destinations`, `bookable` (ADR-IMPL-068: what a tap anywhere on the globe may open from a home airport — the site's cities by their main airport and the US airports the site lists a price from, with the caller's `also` (Home's fares' and offers' arrivals), never home nor an airport under 100 km from it; null while `catalog.globe_tap` is off: only the flag's row, through the flags' cache, nothing of the set), `searchAirports`, `getAirport`, `getAirports`, `indicativeFor` (the estimate an undated search shows for a route, or null — requests asks it for a quote, ADR-IMPL-042), `siteDeals` (the site's deals a home sees, ADR-IMPL-067), `importCsv`.
**Routes:** `GET /v1/search` (rate rule `search`; only fares on sale now — published, `valid_from ≤ now < valid_until`, as `/v1/fares/:id` reads them; `when` is the travel day: a timed fare by its departure day in the origin's zone, an undated one while still sold that day; a `when` before yesterday or past two years ahead lists nothing, without a query; `when` not a date or `from`/`to` not three letters → 400 — ADR-IMPL-041 amendment), `GET /v1/fares/:id` (410 if past `valid_until` or unpublished, 404 for an id that is not a UUID, rate rule `read`), `GET /v1/airports?q=` (max 8, rate rule `read`), `GET /v1/airports/popular?from=` and `GET /v1/airports/home-suggestion?tz=` (rate rule `read`), `GET /v1/places/photos?codes=` (1–20 IATA codes, rate rule `read`), `GET /v1/places/:code/landmarks` (rate rule `read`; ETag, 304; 404 for a code that is no airport — ADR-IMPL-064), `POST /v1/internal/catalog/import` (`catalog:import`), `PUT` and `DELETE /v1/internal/places/:code/photo` (`catalog:import`), `GET /v1/internal/demand?days=7` (`ops:read`, no rate limit).
**Jobs:** `retention-demand` (daily, ADR-IMPL-077: `demand_daily` after 400 days, in batches, `retainEach`), `refresh-site-prices` every 10 minutes (ADR-IMPL-063) — with `catalog.estimates`, `catalog.site_prices` and `catalog.site_pins` on (read again before each question: one turned off stops the run, `stopped: 1`), the site's answers for the routes Home's site pins show: from each airport `site_routes` lists, its first 36 cities in the pins' order, round trip; no stored answer first, then those answered 18 hours ago or more; at most 150 questions a run, one every 500 ms; it stops at the site's first 429 and when the breaker opens, stores nothing for an error — a route the site failed waits an hour (`backingOff`), then goes after the routes never asked and the due ones, the failures kept in the process as its breaker is — then deletes the expired rows; with a flag off it asks nothing and still deletes them (`{ skipped: 1, purged }`); singleton, 5 minutes at most; a route it stored is not asked again until it is due, so repeating a run rewrites no answer that is not due — while more than 150 routes need asking (an environment's first runs), each run takes the next 150. `expire-fares` every 15 minutes — sets `published = false` where `valid_until < now()`, then clears the destinations cache. `demand-rollup` at 03:15 UTC writes yesterday's sketches into `demand_daily`. `resolve-place-photos` every minute — up to ten due cities (ADR-IMPL-043), its requests ending within 40 s of its start (each may take 8 s; timeout 50 s): a city it has not reached by then waits for the next run, uncounted (ADR-IMPL-064). `resolve-place-landmarks` every five minutes — up to two due places, timeout 240 s, a place starting only while its slowest look (162 s: two landmark questions, the city found again, two Commons questions) can end within 225 s of the run's start, else waiting untouched (`later`); nothing with `catalog.landmarks` off or a Wikimedia source held (ADR-IMPL-064). `sync-site-destinations` at 07:40 and 19:40 UTC — the site's cities, their airports, photos and deals (ADR-IMPL-045; twice a day since ADR-IMPL-067, so one failed run leaves the deals twelve more hours of their day); nothing asked with `catalog.site_photos`, `catalog.site_pins`, `catalog.site_offers` and `catalog.globe_tap` off (ADR-IMPL-068: the globe's tap reads the snapshot); every route it writes is served until `now() + 24 h`, a city whose page fails keeps its rows with their own day; the 80 % guard weighs a night against the snapshot only while its day has more than an hour to run (`siteRepo.guarding`), so a site that lastingly lists fewer cities is taken a day after its last good read. The site's API allows 120 requests a minute: its ~241 pages start 1.2 s apart across the four workers (about 50 a minute, so staging's read at the same minute fits beside it), a 429 holds them all for its `Retry-After` (60 s without one, at most 120 s) and the page is asked once more, a second 429 fails the night; photo checks are not paced. About five to six minutes a run, staging's read beside it included (the 120 are the address's); timeout 600 s (ADR-IMPL-062). The site's photo rows are known by either address, today's (`SITE_STORAGE`) or `weba.`'s (`LEGACY_SITE_STORAGE`): the night moves an old one or hands it back.
**A dated search (ADR-IMPL-077)** compares `depart_at` with the travel day's two instants in the origin's zone, computed by Postgres in the same statement from the zone of the airport in memory (`dayStartIn`; checked against Postgres's own day for every airport zone and two years of days, `scripts/tz-parity.test.ts`, `bun run test:tz`), never a function of the column, whenever the origin has a valid IANA zone (`isIanaZone`); one without falls back to the day of `depart_at AT TIME ZONE` its zone in SQL (UTC when it has none, `fares.repo.ts`). A fare's clocks and day (`departLocal`, `arriveLocal`, `departDate`; the request draft's day) are read in the same select by Postgres in its airports' zones (`wallAt`, #114) — the catalogue keeps no copy of the zones' rules; "Popular from" reads one origin's days on `demand_daily_from_day` (0049). **Airports in memory (ADR-IMPL-075):** each process holds `catalog.airports` whole (`application/airports-reference.ts`), loaded again when the `catalog.airports` generation moves (the table's trigger: an import, the reference load, psql) — `getAirport`/`getAirports` without an executor, the hubs, a zone's busiest airports, every route's airport by code. The typeahead (ADR-IMPL-036's query, unchanged) keeps each typed text's answer for the generation, at most 5 000 texts. A caller's transaction reads the table. **Home map:** `destinations()` is `DISTINCT ON (route_to)` ordered by price, kept per home for 60 s and for one generation of `catalog.fares` and `catalog.airports` (`platform.reference.memo`), so an import or expire-fares empties it on every replica within a second. A fare that expires mid-minute can stay on the map for ≤ 60s; its detail answers 410. After the fares' pins, with `catalog.site_pins` and estimates on, up to twelve of the site's cities listed from home (`site_routes`, only rows with more than 141 s of their day left, as the deals), priced by the formula in the member's cabin and kept the same way, on the `catalog.site` generation (the site's places and routes; its stored prices have no trigger and are seen within the minute) (ADR-IMPL-045). With `catalog.site_prices` on (ADR-IMPL-063) a site pin shows the site's stored answer (`priceSource: "site"`), read in one batch, else the formula (`priceSource: "club"`) — never a call to the site — and a city the company has no price for gets no pin; the key gains `:site`. ADR-IMPL-068: with `catalog.globe_tap` on, Home also sends `bookable` — the site's cities and origins (one UNION read of the snapshot, the same for every member, kept a minute and dropped with the pins), unioned in memory with Home's fares' and offers' arrivals, home and its metro left out.
**Site deals** (ADR-IMPL-067): with `catalog.site_offers` on (seeded off; it needs neither the pins nor estimates — a deal carries the site's own price), `siteDeals(exec, home, limit)` answers the site's deals for Home's "Offers to inspire", from the snapshot, never from the site. The site's origins are metros (`SITE_ORIGIN_AIRPORTS`: New York JFK EWR LGA, Washington, Chicago, Houston, Dallas — named as the site names them), else one airport named by its city; the member's own metro first, then each other by great-circle distance to its nearest airport (`application/site-deals.ts`), so Seattle, Toronto and Chișinău get the nearest city's deals, and Boston its five, then New York's. Each metro's deals in the pins' order (the site's home page first, then the destination's traffic), each city's from the metro's preferred airport (home's, then the metro's order); one deal per destination city — the nearest origin's — never the member's own city (an airport of it, or one under 100 km), at most `limit`. A deal: `site-<deal>-<airport>`, the destination as an airport with its city's place photo (with `catalog.place_photos` on; the same photo and credit `/v1/places/photos` serves), the origin `{ code, city }`, `{ amount, USD, basis: "site", from: true }` — the site's "Price Today"; its "Old Price" is never read for a deal — and `validUntil`; for the BFF also the city's and the origin metro's airports. Only rows served 141 s from now (`valid_until > now() + 141 s`, `keptMargin`: the memo's minute and the minute Home's 304 can serve a body after it, 10 % more — the margin Redis's spread expiries needed before ADR-IMPL-075, kept so the figures do not move — and the read itself): a deal is never shown past its day while Home keeps it. Reads: the home airport, the origins (grouped), one read of the nearest metros' deals (another only when they overlap past what they hold); kept a minute in each process per `{HOME}:{limit}:{photos}`, 300 homes at most (about 56 KB each, measured), and for one generation of `catalog.site` and `catalog.airports` (`platform.reference.memo`, ADR-IMPL-075), so the sync or an airports import empties it on every replica within a second; with an executor, read now, unkept.
**Estimates:** `GET /v1/search` adds `estimate` (ADR-IMPL-037) when `catalog.estimates` is on and an undated search has no fare in the cabin: the company's formula, with the rules from the flag row `catalog.pricing_rules` (written by `scripts/load-pricing-rules.ts`; never in the repository; `--check-site` compares a rule set with the site's search on 24 routes and prints counts, never a price; it passes only when no route differs and every route got the site's business figure). Continents are OurAirports' by country, except where the site prices a country elsewhere: Cyprus, St Barthélemy and Saint Helena as Europe, Timor-Leste as Oceania; Turkey is Asia, as on the site (ADR-IMPL-062). Missing, hand-edited or unreadable rules → no estimate and `bbc_search_estimates_unavailable{reason}` (`missing`, `invalid`, `error`: the row could not be read, logged `pricing rules unreadable`); shown ones count in `bbc_search_estimates_shown{cabin}`. `indicativeFor` applies the same flag, rules, formula and fare rule for a quote request whose app showed the estimate, returns the rules' fingerprint with it, and counts missing or hand-edited rules in `bbc_request_estimates_unavailable{reason}` (the site's pins, ADR-IMPL-045, in `bbc_home_estimates_unavailable{reason}`) (the requests module counts what it stores, `bbc_request_estimates_stored{cabin}`); with estimates off, or no valid rules, it reads nothing; otherwise one read of the route's fares, whether one is on sale (`anyOnSaleSelect`, on the route's index, without the clocks a search computes). A killed catalog still answers it: `catalog.estimates` off is the switch. **The site's price** (ADR-IMPL-063, D6), with `catalog.site_prices` on (seeded off, read under `catalog.estimates`): a published fare, then the site's stored answer (`site_prices`, less than 24 hours old), then — an undated search only — one live question within a second, which stores its answer, then the formula only where the site could not be asked (breaker, timeout, 5xx, 429, a body not its shape, a refused request) or, for the pins and `indicativeFor`, nothing is stored; a `none` answer (404 `Offer not found` / `Not found`) is no number at all. One airport, one metro or no end in North America: nothing, and nothing asked. The cabin rules hold for the site's figures (first only above business). `EstimateVM.source` (`site` | `club`) and `asOf` say who priced it — absent while the flag is off, so every answer is then exactly as before; `indicativeFor` returns the rules' fingerprint only with the formula's number. The breaker: five failures in a row open it for 60 s, then one call goes. Metrics `bbc_site_prices_answered{status}`, `bbc_site_prices_unavailable{reason}`, `bbc_search_site_prices{source,how}`.
**Discovery** (ADR-IMPL-039): `popular` returns at most four destinations from `from` — the routes searched at least 5 times in the last seven UTC days (`demand_daily`, all cabins together), busiest first, then the hubs by `popularity`; never the origin or an airport under 100 km from it. Names only: a count never leaves the server. Kept 5 minutes in each process per `FROM` and for one generation of `catalog.airports` (`platform.reference.memo`, ADR-IMPL-075), Redis or not: one query (the searches) on a miss, the airports from memory. Until `catalog.search_events` is on and `demand-rollup` has run, it shows the hubs alone. `bbc_popular_destinations_shown{source="searches"|"hubs"}` counts what was shown. `home-suggestion` returns the busiest airport whose `tz` is the phone's zone or that zone's current name (`ZONE_ALIASES`: Android reports ICU names such as `Asia/Calcutta`) — a tie on popularity goes to the zone's own city (`America/Lima` → LIM, not Arequipa; its airports by code), and a tie between other cities, none the zone's own, suggests nothing (`null`, onboarding asks); `null` for UTC or an offset; 400 when `tz` is not a time zone.
**Place photos** (ADR-IMPL-043): `GET /v1/places/photos` answers from `place_photos` at once — `photo` (a card's and a full-width address, and the credit: author, licence, the photo's page; `club` for an operator's photo, null when it needs none), `satellite` (the point the app centres a Mapbox satellite view on: the city's centre, else the airport) or `none` (not looked up yet: the app shows its own image); an unknown code is left out, and a known airport never asked about gets a `pending` row in the same request (one INSERT … SELECT; on conflict a no-op update returns the row another request added, so it is answered too). `resolve-place-photos` claims up to ten due rows (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, new rows first, a ten-minute hold), asks Wikidata once (P238 at any rank, so a demoted code such as KIV still counts → the airport within 50 km of ours → P931 the city: P3451 night view, P18 image, P625 centre; with no such airport, or one naming no city, the city by its own name near the airport — Wikidata's search, a settlement within 150 km: ours by its English name, else the search's first (its order: "Kishinev" finds Chișinău before the nearer Chișinău Ghetto), one more question each, two a run, a third deferred; a search that fails is its own city's failure, the cities still waiting for one deferred; a tab or another control character in a city's name is searched as a space: ADR-IMPL-064) and Commons twice (500 and 1280 px, Wikimedia's standard widths; a JPEG — never a TIFF (amendment 3, withdrawn: the one a site city had was a satellite image), PNG, SVG or GIF — landscape, wider than 1280, a free licence (CC0, public domain, CC BY / BY-SA, the Free Art License: ADR-IMPL-064, amendment 2), no restrictions, an author when the licence asks), then Pexels for at most three cities (`<city> skyline`, a description naming the city and no person); else the satellite view. A failed source (Commons' HTTP-200 errors included) leaves the row as it was and tries again after `least(attempts, 24)` hours — never a downgrade — and so does a claimed row whose airport does not come back (`withoutOutcome`, stage `airport`). A 429 from Wikidata or Commons is not a failure: the cities wait for its Retry-After, the look not counted, and the source is held in `source_holds` (a run that claims cities while a hold is on asks nothing and puts them back until it ends; ADR-IMPL-064). A key Pexels refuses (401) counts as none for the run — a photo shown stays, a city without one gets a day of satellite view; every resolved row is looked at again after 30–33 days. An operator's photo (`PUT /v1/internal/places/:code/photo`) never expires and no job write touches it (`source IS DISTINCT FROM 'override'` on each one); `DELETE` hands the city back. **A landmark stands in** (ADR-IMPL-064, amendment 1): with `catalog.landmarks` on, a city the route would answer with its satellite view is answered `photo` with its first landmark's photograph — the lowest rank among the place's `landmark` rows without the flag's `skip` (`placeLandmarksRepo.standIns`, one `DISTINCT ON` query for the codes on their satellite view, however many; the flag, cached 30 s, is read only when a code is) — its 1280-px thumbnail full width, the same file at 500 px for a card (`commonsThumbAt`; the 1280 one when the address is not a standard thumbnail's), credited `wikimedia` with that photograph's author (its credit line read back, `landmarkAuthor`), licence and Commons page (`commonsPage`, the landmarks' own credit link). Composed when read (`withLandmarkPhotos`), never written: the row stays `satellite` and the job keeps looking; a photo of the city's own — Commons', Pexels', the operator's — stays first; a city not looked up yet stays `none` (phones ask about it again within a minute but keep a photo for twelve hours, which would hide the city's own photo found a minute later); the flag off (nothing read) or the landmark skipped changes the server's next answer, and reaches a phone that already shows the stand-in only when its copy is twelve hours old or the app restarts (the route has no revalidation). Flag `catalog.place_photos` (seeded on) off: the route answers `{ items: [] }` and the job asks nothing. Metrics `bbc_place_photos_resolved{status,source}`, `bbc_place_photos_failed{stage}`, `bbc_place_photos_held{stage}`, `bbc_place_photos_requested`.
**Landmarks** (ADR-IMPL-064): `GET /v1/places/:code/landmarks` answers from `place_landmarks` at once — up to three `{ name, imageUrl, width, height, credit: { text, licence, licenceUrl?, pageUrl } }`, best first (`pageUrl`: the photograph's own page on Commons, from its stored file, which the credit links to — `commonsPage`, as the stand-in's credit), without the items the flag's `skip` lists; none while `catalog.landmarks` (seeded off, `skip: ["Q177174"]`) is off. `resolve-place-landmarks` takes up to two due places — airports with a city photo row or the site's cities' main airports, new ones first — finds each city's Wikidata item (the one its rows know — looked up again when it answers nothing at all, as a merged item does — else as the city photo does; a search by name that fails stops the run with a `failed` marker for its place alone), asks WDQS once per city (items within 20 km of the city item's P625 that are a tourist attraction, a landmark or a museum by class, or heritage; no populated place, never the city; the forty with most sitelinks, with `hint:Query hint:optimizer "None"`; 65-s timeout) and Commons at 1280 px (one question per 50 files, a hundred files at most), and keeps, among the first twenty, each item's first file — P18, else P3451, else P8592 — that is a JPEG at least 1600 px wide, 1.2–2.4 wide for 1 high, not named as a map, logo, flag or coat of arms, under ADR-IMPL-043's credit rules (`commonsCredit`; the Free Art License among them, its address on https: amendment 2), and neither the city's own photo nor another landmark's; three at most, fewer when fewer qualify. Rows per place: `landmark` at ranks 1–3; a `fewer` marker after the last one found (the job does not ask again for 30–33 days); a `failed` marker (rank 1) for a place no look has worked for yet (six hours). A 429 holds its source (`source_holds`) for its Retry-After and stops the run; another failure keeps what a place has, looks again in six hours and stops the run. Twice is the same rows (upsert by rank). Metrics `bbc_place_landmarks_resolved{found}`, `bbc_place_landmarks_failed{stage}`, `bbc_place_landmarks_held{stage}`.
**Trip and description** (ADR-IMPL-056, 0031): `fares.trip` (`catalog.fare_trip`: `round_trip` | `one_way`, default `one_way`) is what a fare's price is for — round trip only where the catalogue says so (images 04 and 13 print `round trip` beside a fare); the fares import's optional `trip` column says it per row, a file without the column or an empty cell says one way, and any other value refuses the import; the trip is part of a fare's identity (0034): a fare is one per route, cabin, carrier, valid_from and trip, so one route sold one way and round trip is two fares, and a fare without a carrier is one fare too (`fares_route_cabin_carrier`, UNIQUE NULLS NOT DISTINCT, the import's ON CONFLICT target; an empty carrier cell is none); `FareVM.trip` carries it. `airports.description` is a destination's one line under its city (04), null until the airports import's optional `description` column gives one (CHECK: 1–160 characters once trimmed; a longer cell refuses the import, an empty one clears the line, a file without the column keeps every airport's line); `AirportVM.description` carries it wherever an airport is served — the search's `from` and `to`, `/v1/airports`, `popular`, `home-suggestion`, and Home's `home` and pins (`DestinationPinVM`, the fares' and the site's) — and Home's ETag follows it.
**Import** (`POST /v1/internal/catalog/import`): the CSV is read as RFC 4180 (quoted commas, `""`, quoted line breaks; each record keeps the file line it starts on, the header is line 1). Every row is checked before anything is written, in one transaction: a bad file writes nothing and answers 400 `VALIDATION` with up to twenty `details` `{ path: "row N.<field>", message }`; a row the database still refuses is named `row N` with its constraint, never the query. Fares: route codes three letters, different, and airports the catalogue knows (one lookup); price and published price digits with at most two decimals, above 0, published ≥ price and with its source; currency three letters (upper-cased; empty is USD); an empty optional cell (carrier name, product, published price and source) is none; valid_until after valid_from; duration_minutes whole minutes above 0 (empty is none; CHECK `fares_duration_pos`, 0035); nonstop yes or no in any case (`TRUE`/`yes`/`y`/`1`, `FALSE`/`no`/`n`/`0`; empty is nonstop), anything else refused. Airports: code three letters, country code two, lat −90…90, lng −180…180 (CHECK `airports_coordinates`, 0035), popularity a whole number from 0 (empty is 0), region one the catalogue already uses (Home's keys), tz a zone Postgres reads, stored as Postgres spells it (`europe/london` is `Europe/London`; `@bbc/db/time-zones`): a search and a fare's page read it there (#114), so one Postgres's tzdata lacks (`US/Pacific-New`, which ICU still takes) is refused — it failed every dated search from the airport.
**Out of scope:** promotional offers (`proposals.offers`), Sabre adapter, mobile wiring.
**Invariants tested:** search JFK→LHR business → ≥3 · expired fare → 410 · airports `q=JF` ranks JFK · import idempotent, a fare without a carrier included · one route's one-way and round-trip fares are two fares · a fare is one way unless its import row says round trip, an unknown trip refuses the import · a destination's line reaches every airport answer and Home past its ETag, an import without the column keeps it, an empty cell clears it, over 160 characters refuses it (and the CHECK refuses a blank one) · unauthenticated → 401 · timed fixture `arrive − depart == duration` · fa01 maps to 18:55 / 07:00 / +1 · the same clocks and day, Postgres's, under a session time zone of Europe/Chisinau and of America/Los_Angeles (`day-bounds.test.ts`) · every `airports.csv` `tz` is IANA. Search events reject `memberId`, `ip`, and `deviceId`. Estimates: only without fares, never between one metro's airports or for a country in `UNCONFIRMED_COUNTRIES` (empty since ADR-IMPL-062), first only above business; Istanbul priced as Asia; the engine reproduces the reference results (`tests/parity`, CI secret; the club's engine since ADR-IMPL-062). The site's price (ADR-IMPL-063): its answers read by status and body (200 by shape, the two 404s as none, any other 404 or shape invalid, 400, 429, 5xx, a failed connection, a timeout) · the breaker opens after five failures, lets one call through after a minute, a none is no failure, a call its caller stopped neither · stored, then asked once, then the formula only when the site could not be asked · a none is no number, stored or just asked · the guards ask nothing · a quote and the pins never ask · a stored answer is replaced, never doubled; 25 hours refused by the CHECK; an expired one never read, then purged; Home's pins, cached a minute, never read one within its last minute · with the flag off the search, the pins, the quote and the job are as before and nothing is asked · the job: the pins' routes once, 500 ms apart, a second run asks no route it stored and leaves the same rows, a due route asked again, stops at a 429 and when the breaker opens, asks nothing without the site's pins, a route the site keeps failing waits its hour and never holds back the routes after it, stops before its next question when any of its three flags is turned off, deletes expired rows with the flag off · Option A: an app built before `source` keeps the number.
