# domain/catalog

**Owns:** schema `catalog.*` — `fares` (route/cabin/price catalogue, with the trip each price is for), `airports` (IATA reference for pins + autocomplete, with a destination's one line), `demand_daily` (UTC-day search counts, no member id), `place_photos` (the photo shown for each airport's city: addresses and credit, or a satellite view's centre — never an image; ADR-IMPL-043), `site_places` / `site_routes` (the company site's cities, their airports and the US airports the site lists a price from — a nightly snapshot; ADR-IMPL-045), and `site_prices` (what the site's own search answered for a route and trip — its figures, whole USD, or `none`: the company has no price there — shown for at most 24 hours, CHECK `site_prices_expiry`; ADR-IMPL-063).
**Publishes:** none on the journal. When `catalog.search_events` is on and `KAFKA_BROKERS` is set, `GET /v1/search` notes a route event that a flusher publishes to `bbc.search.v1`.
**Consumes:** `bbc.search.v1` on the worker (`APP_ROLE` not `api`), group `catalog.demand`, idempotent via `platform.kafka_processed`.
**Ports:** none (`needs: []`). Repository surface is the port — a Sabre / company-API adapter can replace `fares.repo` later without route changes. Outside calls: the `resolve-place-photos` job asks Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons and (with `PEXELS_API_KEY`) Pexels; `sync-site-destinations` asks the company's site (`buybusinessclass.com/api` and its `/storage/`; ADR-IMPL-062); `scripts/load-pricing-rules.ts --check-site` asks the site's search (`webapi.buybusinessclass.com/flights/search`, `pricing/site-check.ts`); with `catalog.site_prices` on, an undated search without a stored answer asks it once (1 s) and `refresh-site-prices` asks it for the pins' routes (10 s each), through one client per process with a breaker (`application/site-prices.ts`, ADR-IMPL-063); `catalogModule({ fetch, siteClock })` lets tests answer for them, and pace the site's API, the price job and the breaker without waiting.
**Facade:** `searchFares` (at most 30), `getFare`, `destinations`, `bookable` (ADR-IMPL-068: what a tap anywhere on the globe may open from a home airport — the site's cities by their main airport and the US airports the site lists a price from, with the caller's `also` (Home's fares' and offers' arrivals), never home nor an airport under 100 km from it; null while `catalog.globe_tap` is off: only the flag's row, through the flags' cache, nothing of the set), `searchAirports`, `getAirport`, `getAirports`, `indicativeFor` (the estimate an undated search shows for a route, or null — requests asks it for a quote, ADR-IMPL-042), `importCsv`.
**Routes:** `GET /v1/search` (rate rule `search`; only fares on sale now — published, `valid_from ≤ now < valid_until`, as `/v1/fares/:id` reads them; `when` is the travel day: a timed fare by its departure day in the origin's zone, an undated one while still sold that day; a `when` before yesterday or past two years ahead lists nothing, without a query; `when` not a date or `from`/`to` not three letters → 400 — ADR-IMPL-041 amendment), `GET /v1/fares/:id` (410 if past `valid_until` or unpublished, 404 for an id that is not a UUID, rate rule `read`), `GET /v1/airports?q=` (max 8, rate rule `read`), `GET /v1/airports/popular?from=` and `GET /v1/airports/home-suggestion?tz=` (rate rule `read`), `GET /v1/places/photos?codes=` (1–20 IATA codes, rate rule `read`), `POST /v1/internal/catalog/import` (`catalog:import`), `PUT` and `DELETE /v1/internal/places/:code/photo` (`catalog:import`), `GET /v1/internal/demand?days=7` (`ops:read`, no rate limit).
**Jobs:** `refresh-site-prices` every 10 minutes (ADR-IMPL-063) — with `catalog.estimates`, `catalog.site_prices` and `catalog.site_pins` on (read again before each question: one turned off stops the run, `stopped: 1`), the site's answers for the routes Home's site pins show: from each airport `site_routes` lists, its first 36 cities in the pins' order, round trip; no stored answer first, then those answered 18 hours ago or more; at most 150 questions a run, one every 500 ms; it stops at the site's first 429 and when the breaker opens, stores nothing for an error — a route the site failed waits an hour (`backingOff`), then goes after the routes never asked and the due ones, the failures kept in the process as its breaker is — then deletes the expired rows; with a flag off it asks nothing and still deletes them (`{ skipped: 1, purged }`); singleton, 5 minutes at most; a route it stored is not asked again until it is due, so repeating a run rewrites no answer that is not due — while more than 150 routes need asking (an environment's first runs), each run takes the next 150. `expire-fares` every 15 minutes — sets `published = false` where `valid_until < now()`, then clears the destinations cache. `demand-rollup` at 03:15 UTC writes yesterday's sketches into `demand_daily`. `resolve-place-photos` every minute — up to ten due cities (ADR-IMPL-043). `sync-site-destinations` at 07:40 UTC — the site's cities, their airports and photos (ADR-IMPL-045); nothing asked with `catalog.site_photos`, `catalog.site_pins` and `catalog.globe_tap` off (ADR-IMPL-068: the globe's tap reads the snapshot). The site's API allows 120 requests a minute: its ~241 pages start 1.2 s apart across the four workers (about 50 a minute, so staging's read at the same minute fits beside it), a 429 holds them all for its `Retry-After` (60 s without one, at most 120 s) and the page is asked once more, a second 429 fails the night; photo checks are not paced. About five to six minutes a night, staging's read beside it included (the 120 are the address's); timeout 600 s (ADR-IMPL-062). The site's photo rows are known by either address, today's (`SITE_STORAGE`) or `weba.`'s (`LEGACY_SITE_STORAGE`): the night moves an old one or hands it back.
**Home map:** `destinations()` is `DISTINCT ON (route_to)` ordered by price. Without Redis, a 60s process cache. With Redis, key `catalog:dest:{generation}:{HOME}` and `INCR catalog:dest:gen` on import and expire-fares, so every home misses without a SCAN. Airports use `catalog:airports:gen` the same way. A fare that expires mid-minute can stay on the map for ≤ 60s; its detail answers 410. After the fares' pins, with `catalog.site_pins` and estimates on, up to twelve of the site's cities listed from home (`site_routes`), priced by the formula in the member's cabin and cached the same way (`catalog:site:{generation}:{HOME}:{cabin}:{rules}`); the sync bumps the generation (ADR-IMPL-045). With `catalog.site_prices` on (ADR-IMPL-063) a site pin shows the site's stored answer (`priceSource: "site"`), read in one batch, else the formula (`priceSource: "club"`) — never a call to the site — and a city the company has no price for gets no pin; the key gains `:site`. ADR-IMPL-068: with `catalog.globe_tap` on, Home also sends `bookable` — the site's cities and origins (one UNION read of the snapshot, the same for every member, cached a minute as `catalog:bookable:{generation}` or in the process, dropped with the pins), unioned in memory with Home's fares' and offers' arrivals, home and its metro left out.
**Estimates:** `GET /v1/search` adds `estimate` (ADR-IMPL-037) when `catalog.estimates` is on and an undated search has no fare in the cabin: the company's formula, with the rules from the flag row `catalog.pricing_rules` (written by `scripts/load-pricing-rules.ts`; never in the repository; `--check-site` compares a rule set with the site's search on 24 routes and prints counts, never a price; it passes only when no route differs and every route got the site's business figure). Continents are OurAirports' by country, except where the site prices a country elsewhere: Cyprus, St Barthélemy and Saint Helena as Europe, Timor-Leste as Oceania; Turkey is Asia, as on the site (ADR-IMPL-062). Missing, hand-edited or unreadable rules → no estimate and `bbc_search_estimates_unavailable{reason}` (`missing`, `invalid`, `error`: the row could not be read, logged `pricing rules unreadable`); shown ones count in `bbc_search_estimates_shown{cabin}`. `indicativeFor` applies the same flag, rules, formula and fare rule for a quote request whose app showed the estimate, returns the rules' fingerprint with it, and counts missing or hand-edited rules in `bbc_request_estimates_unavailable{reason}` (the site's pins, ADR-IMPL-045, in `bbc_home_estimates_unavailable{reason}`) (the requests module counts what it stores, `bbc_request_estimates_stored{cabin}`); with estimates off, or no valid rules, it reads nothing. A killed catalog still answers it: `catalog.estimates` off is the switch. **The site's price** (ADR-IMPL-063, D6), with `catalog.site_prices` on (seeded off, read under `catalog.estimates`): a published fare, then the site's stored answer (`site_prices`, less than 24 hours old), then — an undated search only — one live question within a second, which stores its answer, then the formula only where the site could not be asked (breaker, timeout, 5xx, 429, a body not its shape, a refused request) or, for the pins and `indicativeFor`, nothing is stored; a `none` answer (404 `Offer not found` / `Not found`) is no number at all. One airport, one metro or no end in North America: nothing, and nothing asked. The cabin rules hold for the site's figures (first only above business). `EstimateVM.source` (`site` | `club`) and `asOf` say who priced it — absent while the flag is off, so every answer is then exactly as before; `indicativeFor` returns the rules' fingerprint only with the formula's number. The breaker: five failures in a row open it for 60 s, then one call goes. Metrics `bbc_site_prices_answered{status}`, `bbc_site_prices_unavailable{reason}`, `bbc_search_site_prices{source,how}`.
**Discovery** (ADR-IMPL-039): `popular` returns at most four destinations from `from` — the routes searched at least 5 times in the last seven UTC days (`demand_daily`, all cabins together), busiest first, then the hubs by `popularity`; never the origin or an airport under 100 km from it. Names only: a count never leaves the server. With Redis, cached 5 minutes under `catalog:popular:{airports gen}:{FROM}`. Until `catalog.search_events` is on and `demand-rollup` has run, it shows the hubs alone. `bbc_popular_destinations_shown{source="searches"|"hubs"}` counts what was shown. `home-suggestion` returns the busiest airport whose `tz` is the phone's zone or that zone's current name (`ZONE_ALIASES`: Android reports ICU names such as `Asia/Calcutta`) — a tie on popularity goes to the zone's own city (`America/Lima` → LIM, not Arequipa; its airports by code), and a tie between other cities, none the zone's own, suggests nothing (`null`, onboarding asks); `null` for UTC or an offset; 400 when `tz` is not a time zone.
**Place photos** (ADR-IMPL-043): `GET /v1/places/photos` answers from `place_photos` at once — `photo` (a card's and a full-width address, and the credit: author, licence, the photo's page; `club` for an operator's photo, null when it needs none), `satellite` (the point the app centres a Mapbox satellite view on: the city's centre, else the airport) or `none` (not looked up yet: the app shows its own image); an unknown code is left out, and a known airport never asked about gets a `pending` row in the same request (one INSERT … SELECT; on conflict a no-op update returns the row another request added, so it is answered too). `resolve-place-photos` claims up to ten due rows (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, new rows first, a ten-minute hold), asks Wikidata once (P238 → the airport within 50 km of ours → P931 the city: P3451 night view, P18 image, P625 centre) and Commons twice (500 and 1280 px, Wikimedia's standard widths; JPEG, landscape, wider than 1280, free licence, no restrictions, an author when the licence asks), then Pexels for at most three cities (`<city> skyline`, a description naming the city and no person); else the satellite view. A failed source (Commons' HTTP-200 errors included) leaves the row as it was and tries again after `least(attempts, 24)` hours — never a downgrade — and so does a claimed row whose airport does not come back (`withoutOutcome`, stage `airport`); a key Pexels refuses (401) counts as none for the run — a photo shown stays, a city without one gets a day of satellite view; every resolved row is looked at again after 30–33 days. An operator's photo (`PUT /v1/internal/places/:code/photo`) never expires and no job write touches it (`source IS DISTINCT FROM 'override'` on each one); `DELETE` hands the city back. Flag `catalog.place_photos` (seeded on) off: the route answers `{ items: [] }` and the job asks nothing. Metrics `bbc_place_photos_resolved{status,source}`, `bbc_place_photos_failed{stage}`, `bbc_place_photos_requested`.
**Trip and description** (ADR-IMPL-056, 0031): `fares.trip` (`catalog.fare_trip`: `round_trip` | `one_way`, default `one_way`) is what a fare's price is for — round trip only where the catalogue says so (images 04 and 13 print `round trip` beside a fare); the fares import's optional `trip` column says it per row, a file without the column or an empty cell says one way, and any other value refuses the import; the trip is part of a fare's identity (0034): a fare is one per route, cabin, carrier, valid_from and trip, so one route sold one way and round trip is two fares, and a fare without a carrier is one fare too (`fares_route_cabin_carrier`, UNIQUE NULLS NOT DISTINCT, the import's ON CONFLICT target; an empty carrier cell is none); `FareVM.trip` carries it. `airports.description` is a destination's one line under its city (04), null until the airports import's optional `description` column gives one (CHECK: 1–160 characters once trimmed; a longer cell refuses the import, an empty one clears the line, a file without the column keeps every airport's line); `AirportVM.description` carries it wherever an airport is served — the search's `from` and `to`, `/v1/airports`, `popular`, `home-suggestion`, and Home's `home` and pins (`DestinationPinVM`, the fares' and the site's) — and Home's ETag follows it.
**Import** (`POST /v1/internal/catalog/import`): the CSV is read as RFC 4180 (quoted commas, `""`, quoted line breaks; each record keeps the file line it starts on, the header is line 1). Every row is checked before anything is written, in one transaction: a bad file writes nothing and answers 400 `VALIDATION` with up to twenty `details` `{ path: "row N.<field>", message }`; a row the database still refuses is named `row N` with its constraint, never the query. Fares: route codes three letters, different, and airports the catalogue knows (one lookup); price and published price digits with at most two decimals, above 0, published ≥ price and with its source; currency three letters (upper-cased; empty is USD); an empty optional cell (carrier name, product, published price and source) is none; valid_until after valid_from; duration_minutes whole minutes above 0 (empty is none; CHECK `fares_duration_pos`, 0035); nonstop yes or no in any case (`TRUE`/`yes`/`y`/`1`, `FALSE`/`no`/`n`/`0`; empty is nonstop), anything else refused. Airports: code three letters, country code two, lat −90…90, lng −180…180 (CHECK `airports_coordinates`, 0035), popularity a whole number from 0 (empty is 0), region one the catalogue already uses (Home's keys), tz an IANA zone.
**Out of scope:** promotional offers (`proposals.offers`), Sabre adapter, mobile wiring.
**Invariants tested:** search JFK→LHR business → ≥3 · expired fare → 410 · airports `q=JF` ranks JFK · import idempotent, a fare without a carrier included · one route's one-way and round-trip fares are two fares · a fare is one way unless its import row says round trip, an unknown trip refuses the import · a destination's line reaches every airport answer and Home past its ETag, an import without the column keeps it, an empty cell clears it, over 160 characters refuses it (and the CHECK refuses a blank one) · unauthenticated → 401 · timed fixture `arrive − depart == duration` · fa01 maps to 18:55 / 07:00 / +1 · same clocks under Europe/Chisinau and America/Los_Angeles · every `airports.csv` `tz` is IANA. Search events reject `memberId`, `ip`, and `deviceId`. Estimates: only without fares, never between one metro's airports or for a country in `UNCONFIRMED_COUNTRIES` (empty since ADR-IMPL-062), first only above business; Istanbul priced as Asia; the engine reproduces the reference results (`tests/parity`, CI secret; the club's engine since ADR-IMPL-062). The site's price (ADR-IMPL-063): its answers read by status and body (200 by shape, the two 404s as none, any other 404 or shape invalid, 400, 429, 5xx, a failed connection, a timeout) · the breaker opens after five failures, lets one call through after a minute, a none is no failure, a call its caller stopped neither · stored, then asked once, then the formula only when the site could not be asked · a none is no number, stored or just asked · the guards ask nothing · a quote and the pins never ask · a stored answer is replaced, never doubled; 25 hours refused by the CHECK; an expired one never read, then purged; Home's pins, cached a minute, never read one within its last minute · with the flag off the search, the pins, the quote and the job are as before and nothing is asked · the job: the pins' routes once, 500 ms apart, a second run asks no route it stored and leaves the same rows, a due route asked again, stops at a 429 and when the breaker opens, asks nothing without the site's pins, a route the site keeps failing waits its hour and never holds back the routes after it, stops before its next question when any of its three flags is turned off, deletes expired rows with the flag off · Option A: an app built before `source` keeps the number. The site's API: one request start every `API_GAP_MS` (1.2 s) across the pool, a 429 retried once after its wait, a second one fails the read and stops its workers; a photo stored under the site's former address is moved or handed back. `--check-site`: counts and route codes only. Discovery: searched ≥ 5 times in seven days first, then the hubs · never the origin or its metro · no count in the body · `Asia/Calcutta` → DEL, `Etc/UTC` → none · every airport `tz` is reachable from the name a phone reports. Place photos: a new airport is `none` then a Commons photo with its credit · Pexels only with a key and at most three a run · a source that fails keeps the row and backs off · a claimed city whose airport does not come back backs off the same way · the job never overwrites an operator's photo · unknown codes are left out · flag off → no photos, no lookups · an item an older app cannot read is dropped, not the answer. The globe's tap (ADR-IMPL-068): flag off → null, nothing read, and the night asks the site nothing with the site's flags off too; on alone, the night reads the site · the set is the site's main airports and US origins, never home or its metro · a formula price alone adds nothing (the formula prices over a thousand airports from JFK; the set holds ten) · the caller's arrivals join once each, two cities on one airport give one entry · a city that leaves the site leaves the set at the next sync.
