# ADR-IMPL-033 — The Explore globe

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-10-02 · Superseded in part by ADR-IMPL-035 (Mapbox), 2026-10-05: this drawn globe
remains the fallback — e2e builds, no Mapbox token or native module, a Mapbox failure.

**Context.** Explore shows a flat disc with pins. Figma specifies a rotating orthographic globe with real land, and rotation and zoom are wanted now.

**Decision.** The Explore globe is an orthographic projection of Natural Earth land (world-atlas 2.0.2, public domain), computed with d3-geo 3.1.1 and drawn with react-native-svg, matching Figma 97:435 (London selected), 482:1115 (Globe · Map style) and page 06 · Motion. Three detail levels: 110m under a finger, 50m simplified at 1 px² while rotating, 50m at 0.1 px² when still or zoomed in; the land path may use at most a third of the JS thread. Data is generated by `packages/ui/scripts/globe-land.ts` and checked by a test.

**Why not Mapbox.** The design is orthographic, flat-coloured and label-free. Mapbox would add a perspective camera, its mandatory on-map logo and attribution, a public and a secret token, telemetry in the store privacy forms, and a native build. This globe ships by OTA.

**Revisit** for street-level zoom, terrain or offline tile regions.

**Not in Figma:** drag and pinch (owner's request), zoom 1–3×, tilt ±80°, resume after 4 s idle.

**Open — DECISION (designer):** P3.RM2 shows the selected pin white; Globe · Map style says bronze only. The code follows the Map style.

**Consequence.** Mutable view state lives in `globe-engine.ts`, not in the component, so React Compiler can compile `GlobeFallback` without `useMemo` or refs. e2e builds (`EXPO_PUBLIC_APP_ENV=e2e`) hold the opening position so Maestro can tap `globe.pin.LHR`.
